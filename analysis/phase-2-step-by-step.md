## Step 2.4: Build the Internal Linking Structure

### Why This Matters
Internal links tell Google which pages are most important and how they relate to each other. Right now, the site's internal linking appears sparse and inconsistent.

### The Linking Rules

**Rule 1: Every blog post links to its parent service page**
Add a CTA block at the end of every blog post:
```
Need help with your [platform] taxes?
National Accounts specialises in tax returns for [platform] creators.
[Book a Free Consultation →] (link to service page)
```

**Rule 2: Every service page links to 2–3 related blog posts**
Add a "Related Resources" or "Learn More" section at the bottom of each service page:
```
Related Articles:
• [Blog post 1 title] (link)
• [Blog post 2 title] (link)
• [Blog post 3 title] (link)
```

**Rule 3: Hub pages link to ALL sub-pages**
- `/influencer-services/` must link to: OnlyFans, YouTube, TikTok, Patreon, Twitch (new)
- `/entrepreneur/` must link to: Business Accountants, Tax Accountants, Dropshipping, Property Investing, Bookkeeping

**Rule 4: Use descriptive anchor text**
- Bad: "click here", "learn more", "read this"
- Good: "our OnlyFans tax accountants", "TikTok tax guide for Australian creators", "small business accounting services"
