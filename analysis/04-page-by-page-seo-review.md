# Page-by-Page SEO Review

## Priority Order: Home → Influencer → Entrepreneur → Blogs

---

## 1. HOMEPAGE (`/`)

**Title**: National Accounts | Adelaide's Leading Tax Accountants

### What's Working
- Clear positioning as Adelaide's leading tax accountants
- Services listed: corporate finance, tax planning, cyber security, legal structures, SMSFs, small business accounting, bookkeeping, payroll, internal audits
- Mentions both entrepreneurs and influencers — good dual positioning

### SEO Issues
- **Title tag too generic**: "Adelaide's Leading Tax Accountants" — doesn't differentiate or target specific high-value keywords
- **Missing local SEO schema**: No LocalBusiness structured data
- **No visible H1 hierarchy** optimised for search
- **Services mentioned but not linked**: SMSF, personal tax, internal audits, cyber security — all mentioned but have NO dedicated landing pages
- **No social proof above the fold**: No review count, star rating, or trust badges
- **No author/team credentials visible**: For a YMYL site, this is critical post-December 2025 update

### Recommended Title
`Tax Accountants Adelaide | Influencer & Small Business Accounting — National Accounts`

### Recommended Actions
1. Add LocalBusiness + AccountingService schema markup
2. Add Google Reviews widget or star rating with review count
3. Add professional body logos (CPA, CA, Tax Practitioners Board)
4. Add team photos with qualifications (CA, CPA, registered tax agent numbers)
5. Create dedicated landing pages for: SMSF, Personal Tax, Internal Audits
6. Improve H1 to include primary keyword naturally
7. Add FAQ section with schema for common queries
8. Ensure page loads in under 2.5 seconds (LCP)

## 2. INFLUENCER SERVICES HUB (`/influencer-services/`)

**Title**: Accountants & Tax Services for Influencers

### What's Working
- Good hub structure with sub-pages for each platform
- Covers YouTube, OnlyFans, Twitch, Patreon, TikTok
- Strong niche positioning — few competitors in AU market

### SEO Issues
- **Title tag**: Misses "Australia" — should target "influencer tax accountant Australia"
- **Missing platforms**: No dedicated Twitch service page (despite blog content about Twitch)
- **No pricing indicators**: Even ballpark ranges help conversions and featured snippets
- **No case studies or testimonials**: Critical for E-E-A-T
- **Internal linking**: Blog posts about influencer tax don't consistently link back here
- **No FAQ schema**: Missing easy wins for SERP features

### Recommended Title
`Influencer Tax Accountants Australia | YouTube, OnlyFans, TikTok, Twitch — National Accounts`

### Recommended Actions
1. Add Twitch-specific service page (`/influencer-services/twitch-tax-accountants/`)
2. Add 2–3 anonymised case studies ("Creator earning $X, we saved them $Y")
3. Add FAQ section with schema markup
4. Add pricing guide section (even ranges like "$500–$1,500/year")
5. Add testimonials from real creators (with platform they use)
6. Strengthen internal links FROM all influencer blog posts TO this hub
7. Add "As featured in" or media mentions if any exist
8. Add author bio with tax agent credentials
