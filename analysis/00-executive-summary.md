## Why Traffic Dropped (Mid-December 2025 → Now)

**Primary cause**: Google's December 2025 Core Update (rolled out Dec 11–29) hit the site hard. This is compounded by continued algorithm volatility in January, a February Discover update, and a March 2026 Core Update currently rolling out.

**National Accounts was specifically vulnerable because**:
1. **YMYL site** (accounting/tax = "Your Money, Your Life") — these sites faced a 67% impact rate
2. **Weak E-E-A-T signals** — no visible author credentials, professional body logos, or trust signals
3. **Severe content cannibalisation** — 6 pages competing for "OnlyFans tax", 3 for "TikTok tax", 4 for general "influencer tax"
4. **A literal duplicate page** — two identical "Small Business Accounting" pages at different URLs
5. **Stale content** — service pages from Oct 2024 never updated, sporadic blog publishing
6. **Technical issues** — site blocks crawlers (403), likely sitemap/robots.txt issues
