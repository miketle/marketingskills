- Tax implications

H2: Sole Trader
- What it is
- Pros: simple, cheap, full control
- Cons: unlimited liability, taxed at personal rate
- Best for: freelancers, side hustles, early-stage businesses
- Tax rate: marginal rates up to 45% + Medicare levy
- Setup cost: Free (just an ABN)

H2: Company (Pty Ltd)
- What it is
- Pros: limited liability, 25% tax rate, credibility
- Cons: setup cost, compliance (ASIC), can't access losses personally
- Best for: businesses earning $100K+, those wanting liability protection
- Tax rate: 25% for base rate entities
- Setup cost: $500–$1,500 + ongoing ASIC fees

H2: Trust (Family/Discretionary)
- What it is
- Pros: income splitting, asset protection, CGT flexibility
- Cons: complex, can't retain losses, distribution requirements
- Best for: families, property investors, high earners wanting to distribute income
- Tax rate: distributed to beneficiaries at their marginal rates
- Setup cost: $1,500–$3,000

H2: Partnership
- Brief overview — when it makes sense, limitations

## Content Standards for ALL New Posts

Every new blog post MUST include:

1. **Author bio** — Name, photo, qualifications, registration numbers
2. **Publication date** — visible on page
3. **FAQ section** — 4–6 questions with FAQPage schema markup
4. **Internal links** — minimum 2 links to service pages, 1 link to a related blog post
5. **CTA block** — "Need help? Book a free consultation with National Accounts"
6. **Meta title** — unique, keyword-optimised, under 60 characters
7. **Meta description** — unique, includes keyword + CTA, 150–160 characters
8. **Featured image** — relevant, branded (keep design consistent with existing site)
9. **Word count** — minimum 1,500 words (Google favours comprehensive content for YMYL)
10. **Proper heading hierarchy** — H1 (once) → H2 → H3 (no skipping levels)
