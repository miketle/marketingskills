### Key Observations

**1. Massively Over-Indexed on OnlyFans (31% of all content)**
- 5 posts about OnlyFans tax is excessive for 16 total blog posts
- Creates cannibalisation and suggests the blog strategy is platform-first rather than topic-first
- Many of these posts cover overlapping ground (GST, deadlines, managing payments)

**2. Zero Content for Entrepreneur/Business Audience**
- No blog posts targeting small business owners, tradies, medical professionals, or property investors
- This means the blog ONLY serves the influencer audience — the entrepreneur and industry vertical pages have zero supporting content
- Huge missed opportunity for the higher-value client segment

**3. Sporadic Publishing**
- 2024: ~4 posts
- 2025: ~10 posts
- 2026: 2 posts (W-8BEN in March, Legal Risks in January)
- No consistent cadence — Google rewards fresh, regular publishing

**4. No Evergreen Pillar Content**
- All posts are relatively short, topical pieces
- No comprehensive "ultimate guides" that could serve as pillar content
- No internal linking strategy connecting blogs to service pages

**5. Missing Author Attribution**
- Blog posts don't appear to have visible author bios with credentials
- For YMYL/tax content, Google requires demonstrated expertise
- Every post needs: author name, qualifications (CA/CPA), headshot, link to about page

## Recommended Publishing Cadence

- **Minimum**: 2 posts per month (1 influencer, 1 entrepreneur/business)
- **Ideal**: 4 posts per month (2 influencer, 1 entrepreneur, 1 industry vertical)
- **Every post MUST have**: Author bio with credentials, internal links to 2+ service pages, FAQ schema, clear CTA to contact/book
