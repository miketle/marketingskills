## Content Strategy: Priority Pages to Create

### Tier 1 — Create Immediately (Highest Impact)

#### 1. Division 296 Content Hub
**URL:** `/division-296-smsf-property-valuations/`
**Target keywords:** "division 296 smsf valuation," "division 296 property valuation," "smsf valuation $3 million threshold"

**Why:** Division 296 takes effect July 2026. This is the most time-sensitive opportunity. Acumentis already has a dedicated page. The site has NOTHING on this topic.

**Content outline:**
- What Division 296 means for SMSF property holders
- How property valuations directly affect your Division 296 tax liability
- Why accurate annual valuations matter more than ever
- The risk of understating or overstating property values
- How to prepare before 30 June 2026
- CTA: Order your Division 296-compliant valuation report

#### 2. Residential SMSF Valuations Page
**URL:** `/residential-property-valuations/`
**Target keywords:** "smsf residential property valuation," "smsf house valuation," "smsf property valuation report"

**Why:** The site has extensive commercial content but no dedicated residential page. Residential is likely the larger volume of orders.

#### 3. For Accountants & Auditors Page
**URL:** `/for-accountants/`
**Target keywords:** "smsf property valuation for accountants," "smsf valuation bulk orders," "auditor approved smsf valuation"

**Why:** Accountants and auditors are the primary referral channel. A dedicated B2B page captures this intent and enables partnership SEO.

#### 4. ATO Compliance Guide
**URL:** `/smsf-valuation-ato-requirements/`
**Target keywords:** "smsf valuation ato requirements," "ato smsf property valuation guidelines," "smsf valuation compliance"

**Why:** The ATO's own page ranks #1. A detailed, practitioner-focused guide that cites and explains ATO guidance can rank #2-3 and drive high-intent traffic.

**URL:** `/smsf-related-party-property-valuation/`
**Target keywords:** "smsf related party property valuation," "smsf business real property valuation"

**Why:** Related-party transactions require independent valuations. High compliance urgency = high conversion intent.
