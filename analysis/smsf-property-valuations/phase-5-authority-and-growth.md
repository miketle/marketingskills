## 5.3 Blog Content Calendar

### Monthly Publishing Schedule

Publish 2 posts per month. Focus on SMSF valuation topics that drive search traffic.

| Month | Post 1 | Post 2 |
|---|---|---|
| **April 2026** | Division 296 and Your SMSF Property: What Changes July 2026 | SMSF Valuation 30 June: Your Annual Compliance Checklist |

| **June 2026** | ATO Crackdown on Static SMSF Valuations: Are You at Risk? | Desktop vs Full Valuation for SMSF: Which Do You Need? |
| **July 2026** | Division 296 Is Live: What SMSF Trustees Must Do Now | SMSF Related Party Property Valuation: Rules & Requirements |
| **August 2026** | SMSF Retrospective Property Valuations Explained | SMSF Auditor Contravention Reports: Avoid Valuation Breaches |
| **September 2026** | How SMSF Property Valuations Work: Behind the Scenes | SMSF Vacant Land Valuation: What Trustees Need to Know |

### Blog Post Template

Each post should follow this structure:
1. **H1:** Keyword-rich title
2. **Intro paragraph:** What the post covers, why it matters (100-150 words)
3. **Body:** 3-5 H2 sections with actionable information (800-1,500 words total)
4. **FAQ section:** 3-5 common questions with brief answers (add FAQ schema)
5. **CTA:** "Need an SMSF property valuation? Order from $245" with link to order page
6. **Internal links:** At least 3 links to other pages on the site

### Content Quality Standards

- Written by or reviewed by a qualified valuer (shows E-E-A-T)
- Cite ATO guidelines and legislation where relevant
- Include author bio with qualifications
- Use real examples and scenarios
- No AI-generated filler — every paragraph should add value
- Keep under 2,000 words (concise, actionable)
