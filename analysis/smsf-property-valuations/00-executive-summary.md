## What the Business Does

SMSF Property Valuations provides certified desktop valuation reports for residential and commercial properties held within Self-Managed Super Funds (SMSFs) across Australia. Reports are ATO-compliant and accepted by auditors.

- **Residential valuations:** From $245 | 8–10 page reports | Same-day/next-business-day delivery
- **Commercial valuations:** From $550 | 8–12 page reports | 48-hour delivery
- Also offers: retrospective valuations, CGT valuations, divorce/family law valuations

---

## Key Findings

### Strengths
1. **Exact-match domain** — `smsfpropertyvaluations.com.au` is the ideal domain for this niche
2. **Clear pricing and fast turnaround** — strong competitive advantage
3. **Niche specialisation** — 100% focused on SMSF valuations
4. **Content volume** — 25+ indexed pages covering commercial valuations, calculators, city-specific pages
5. **Timely market tailwind** — Division 296 legislation (effective July 2026) and ATO crackdown on static valuations create surging demand

### Critical Issues
1. **Site blocks crawlers (403 errors)** — robots.txt or server config may be blocking legitimate bots, including search engines and analysis tools
2. **404 errors on old blog URLs** — `/blog/smsf/transfer-property-into-smsf/` returns 404, indicating broken internal links or a botched migration
3. **Duplicate `/home-2/` page** — a second homepage exists at `/home-2/`, creating duplicate content
4. **No Google Business Profile found** — missing a free, high-visibility local SEO channel
5. **No visible reviews/testimonials** — no presence on Google Reviews, Trustpilot, or ProductReview
6. **Missing city pages for Perth, Adelaide, Hobart, Canberra, Darwin** — only Sydney, Melbourne, and Brisbane have dedicated pages
7. **Heavy commercial keyword cannibalisation** — multiple pages targeting near-identical "commercial property valuation" variations
8. **No Division 296 content** — massive missed opportunity given this is the #1 trending topic in SMSF compliance
