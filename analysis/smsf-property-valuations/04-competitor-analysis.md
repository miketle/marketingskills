## Competitive Advantages You Have

1. **Exact-match domain** — `smsfpropertyvaluations.com.au` is the best possible domain for this niche
2. **Transparent pricing** — $245 residential, $550 commercial (most competitors hide pricing)
3. **Fast turnaround** — Same-day/next-day residential, 48hr commercial
4. **Niche focus** — 100% SMSF valuations (unlike Acumentis, Opteon who do everything)
5. **Online ordering** — Easy digital process vs. calling offices
6. **Content volume** — 25+ indexed pages (more than most niche competitors)

## Competitive Disadvantages to Address

1. **No Division 296 content** — Acumentis is already there
2. **No reviews/testimonials visible online** — competitors like Superfund use "100% track record" claims
3. **No Google Business Profile** — missing Maps visibility entirely
4. **No partnerships highlighted** — Acumentis has BGL partnership, Opteon has scale
5. **403 blocking issue** — may be silently losing rankings
6. **Keyword cannibalisation** — commercial content competing with itself

---

## Competitive Strategy Recommendations

### Beat Niche Competitors (Superfund, LocalValuers)
- **More content, faster** — especially Division 296 and city pages
- **Collect reviews aggressively** — first to build a visible review profile wins trust
- **Calculator/tools** — you already have this, promote it more prominently

### Compete with Authority Players (Acumentis, Opteon)
- **Don't try to match their authority** — instead, be the specialist
- **Win on conversion** — transparent pricing, instant ordering, fast delivery
- **Win on content depth** — they write one page per topic; you write comprehensive guides
- **Win on long-tail keywords** — they target head terms; you capture the long tail

### Leverage Informational Competitors (ATO, SMSF Adviser)
- **Link to ATO content** — builds trust and E-E-A-T
- **Cite SMSF Association guidelines** — positions you as compliant
- **Guest post or be quoted** on SMSF Adviser, Accountants Daily (you already have one article there)
